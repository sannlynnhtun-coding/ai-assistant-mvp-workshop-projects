# 🚀 AI-Assisted Digital Product Development Workshop

## Participant Projects

These working MVPs were created by participants during the AI-Assisted Digital Product Development Workshop. Each project demonstrates how AI tools can support product planning, design, development, database integration, security, testing, and deployment.

### 1. Mini SaaS POS

- **Participant Name:** Hein Min Htet
- **Project Name:** Mini SaaS POS
- **Project Description:** A multi-tenant cloud point-of-sale and inventory management platform for retail businesses. It includes product and stock management, checkout, sales history, financial reports, staff roles, and subscription management.
- **Live Demo:** [minisasspos.vercel.app](https://minisasspos.vercel.app/)
- **Source Code:** [github.com/ravendevhub/mini-saas-pos](https://github.com/ravendevhub/mini-saas-pos)
- **Demo Account:** `ravendevhub@gmail.com`
- **Demo Password:** `Saasadmin`

### 2. SACHI Stationery Shop

- **Participant Name:** Thit Thit Zaw
- **Project Name:** SACHI Stationery Shop
- **Project Description:** An online stationery shop where customers can browse products by category, view product details, add items to a cart, and place orders. It also includes an owner portal for managing products, inventory, and customer orders.
- **Live Demo:** [sta-six.vercel.app](https://sta-six.vercel.app/)
- **Source Code:** [github.com/thitthitzaw432-wq/Sta](https://github.com/thitthitzaw432-wq/Sta)
